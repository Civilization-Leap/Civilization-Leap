# Research Contributions, Counterexamples & Alternative Paths

This registry records substantive external contributions to the V1.6 open research challenge.

It is **not a ranking, score, endorsement list, or ownership claim**. Its purpose is attribution, traceability, and making it easy for later researchers to see what has already been tried.

## Attribution principles

1. A contributor's paper, model, code, experiment, note, or alternative theory remains their work.
2. We link to the contributor's original source whenever possible.
3. A successful counterexample will be recorded as a counterexample, not redefined after the fact to protect V1.6.
4. If a contribution causes V1.6 to be revised, narrowed, downgraded, or abandoned, the revision record should identify the contribution that triggered the change.
5. Disagreement does not imply misunderstanding.

## What counts as substantive

Examples include:

- a counterexample that breaks one causal link;
- a theorem or existing result that resolves an open question;
- a mechanism that provides a stable self-first non-closure stopping condition;
- an experiment that distinguishes temporary cooperation from a stopping condition;
- a third starting-point logic with a causal argument;
- a failure result that materially narrows a proposed alternative.

A contribution does not need to agree with the framework to be listed.
