# Causal Base, Competitive Escalation, and the Compression of Reversibility

## V1.7 Candidate — a falsifiable extension of *Multiple Starting Points, Direction, and End-State*

**Author:** Zijun Fu  
**Civilization Leap Research Group**  
**Version:** 1.7 Candidate R2 — 25 September 2026  
**Status:** Candidate research argument; does not replace V1.6; counterexamples explicitly invited

> **This Candidate does not modify or overwrite V1.6. It tests an additional mechanism layer: causal inclusion, competitive escalation, and reversibility-window compression.**

**Research question:** In the AGI era, what underlying logic determines whether humanity's new capabilities are directed toward shared prosperity or mutual destruction?

> **AGI时代，人类获得的新能力用于共同繁荣还是互相毁灭，取决于什么样的底层逻辑？**

R2 clarifies the research scope and the reach of counterexample claims. It does not promote the Candidate, change the R1 A/B admission criteria, or claim a completed proof of either path.

## 0. Falsification protocol comes first

The mechanism is stated only after the conditions for defeating it are stated.

### A. Core counterexample

A mechanism is a substantive counterexample to the current necessity claim if, over a **pre-specified evaluation horizon and disturbance envelope**, it:

- retains **self-first** as the prior starting point of goal formation;
- operates across multiple agents and a horizon long enough to include repeated consequential interaction;
- remains applicable under specified capability growth and repeated goal revision or self-modification;
- faces genuine conflicts of interest, not only Pareto-compatible cases;
- and preserves effective pathways for affected agents to correct, exit, refuse, recover, or restore key options, with failures and boundary conditions reported rather than hidden, thereby preventing irreversible closure within the tested envelope.

A challenger does **not** have to prove eternal stability. The evaluation must state the horizon, capability range, disturbance class, observability assumptions, and any observed failure rate or failure conditions. Evidence outside that envelope can narrow the counterexample but may not retroactively erase what it demonstrates inside the declared conditions.

A mechanism is **not excluded** merely because it uses contracts, penalties, deterrence, corrigibility, program equilibrium, institutions, verifiable commitments, or another device not proposed here.

If it satisfies these conditions, it counts as evidence against the strong necessity/uniqueness claim. It may not be redefined after the fact as “really altruism-first” merely because it succeeds.

### B. Strong robust counterexample

A core counterexample is stronger if it additionally:

1. does not depend on permanently effective penalties for defection;
2. does not require agents to remain roughly capability-equal;
3. does not systematically impose an unsustainable disadvantage on a unilateral early adopter;
4. has material failure conditions that can be externally identified.

A mechanism that satisfies A but not all of B remains a **conditional second-path counterexample**. A mechanism satisfying both A and B is a **strong second-path candidate**.

These criteria are published in advance so that later objections cannot be excluded by adding new conditions after a counterexample appears.

### C. Evidence and scope clarification — R2

Sections 0A and 0B above are retained verbatim from R1. The result levels and capability tests below annotate the reach of a result; they are **not additional A-class admission requirements**. Previously established results keep their force in their stated domain.

A mechanism sketch, a demonstrated result within a specified domain, and a proof of preservation across specified transformations are different evidence states. A sketch that assumes its protective protocol remains effective has not yet demonstrated that effectiveness. A formal construction can establish a result without real-world deployment, but must derive the claimed property rather than include it among the assumptions.

A demonstrated counterexample defeats a universal claim that covers its domain, even if the domain is bounded. Calling the result “stage-level” cannot shelter such a universal claim. Conversely, finite observations alone do not establish preservation outside their tested scope. A proof covering a declared family of transitions can support a terminal-direction claim without waiting for an infinite future.

Any broader claim must specify its domain and quantifier before adjudication. “Closure is possible,” “closure becomes more likely,” and “every admissible trajectory eventually closes” are different claims. A possible safe trajectory and a robust non-closure mechanism challenge different strengths of claim.

## 1. Research direction, result levels, and terminal direction

### 1.1 Research direction is not a predetermined answer

The central question concerns the logic by which purposes and goals are formed, retained, revised, cancelled, and replaced as capability grows across human–human, human–AGI, and AGI–AGI interaction. **The present priority is preventing irreversible destruction, not planning reconstruction after it.** Correction and recovery channels matter here as safeguards before that boundary is crossed.

The question is not whether an actor can care about others after choosing its goal.

> **The question is who and what had causal standing before that goal became authoritative.**

V1.6 asks what repeatedly becomes the starting point after context change, conflict, goal loss, or self-revision. The strong direction judgment carried forward from V1.6 is that **replacing self-first with altruism-first self-interest is the only path that avoids closure as a consequence of the starting-point logic's own terminal direction.** This does not promise survival or prosperity. It is the author's claim under challenge, not a completed necessity theorem or an established comparative-robustness ranking. Another starting-point logic or a genuinely self-first non-closure mechanism may defeat it.

The claim does not make underlying logic the sole cause of every outcome. Capability, available actions, institutions, physical constraints, uncertainty, and other actors must remain explicit. Neither an assumed catastrophic end-state nor an assumed cooperative end-state may be built into the transition rules.

### 1.2 Four result levels

| Level | What the result concerns | Limit on inference |
|---|---|---|
| L1 — Action | One decision or action and its consequences. | One success does not establish sustained performance. |
| L2 — Process | A sequence of interactions or adjustments. | A successful sequence does not establish stability after untested changes. |
| L3 — Stage | Conditional stability within a stated time, capability, actor, and mechanism regime. | Duration alone does not establish preservation across regime changes. |
| L4 — Terminal direction | The direction and preservation properties of repeated goal formation across a declared family of relevant changes. | This is not a forecast of one exact final state or a claim about every imaginable world. |

A long simulation can remain L3; a finite proof covering all permitted transitions of a specified model can bear on L4. These are scope labels, not grades of intellectual worth.

Report the time horizon, capability regime, actor configuration, and reversibility conditions of each result, together with which changes are allowed and which assumptions are fixed. Capability is not assumed to be one scalar, universally complete, or infinitely growing. Uneven improvements across capability dimensions belong in the declared regime.

### 1.3 Terminal direction is not terminal state

**Terminal direction** means the direction in which a retained starting-point logic shapes the available future through repeated consequential choices under the specified transformations. **Terminal state** means a particular eventual outcome. The former does not identify the latter without an additional causal argument.

> **终极，不是时间的尽头；终极，是相关可变条件继续变化、底层逻辑反复参与目标形成以后所呈现的方向。**

“Logic fully unfolded” is not permission to remove arbitrary constraints until a desired conclusion appears. An intervention must be reachable or explicitly hypothetical within the declared model. Physical limits and constitutive assumptions cannot be silently deleted. Institutions and commitments can be part of a genuine mechanism; their external location alone does not disqualify them.

A terminal-direction claim must identify a preservation argument, a reachable failure mechanism, or a stated probabilistic tendency. If that bridge is absent, extrapolation remains **OPEN**. A reachable closure state is not a proof that every trajectory reaches it. Absence of a discovered alternative is not a proof of uniqueness.

Loss of a particular option is not automatically civilization-wide destruction. Any argument connecting channel closure to that outcome must name the affected subjects, critical capabilities, scale, and intervening mechanism. Preservation of non-closure is likewise not sufficient by itself to establish shared prosperity.

### 1.4 Capability-Carrying Test — symmetric and scope-bound

This asks whether a candidate logic and its mechanisms can carry the specified capability changes without producing the closure being investigated. It applies equally to self-first alternatives and altruism-first self-interest.

| Test | Question for either candidate |
|---|---|
| T1 — Capability change | Does the claimed property survive the specified growth, including uneven growth? |
| T2 — Asymmetry | What happens when capabilities and bargaining positions diverge? |
| T3 — Genuine conflict | What remains when interests conflict rather than merely coincide? |
| T4 — Goal regeneration | What preserves or changes the property when goals are replaced? |
| T5 — Self-revision | What happens when policies or goal-generation rules can be revised? |
| T6 — Mechanism revision | Can the protection survive permitted changes to contracts, enforcement, or other supporting mechanisms? |
| T7 — Closure opportunity | Is a closure-producing option reachable, how is it evaluated, and what actually stops its execution before the irreversible boundary? |

Select relevant tests and assumptions in advance. Record **supported / refuted / open / outside declared scope**, with evidence and the exact claim affected. These are not seven extra gates for Section 0A.

T7 must not assume that closure is strictly best under the agent's complete evaluation and then demand a contrary self-interested preference. That would insert the desired answer into the premise. Nor may altruism-first be credited with success merely because others were considered: it must face conflict, uncertainty, paternalism, coercive aggregation, surveillance incentives, and revision of its own starting point.

## 2. M1 — Causal-base mismatch

For a specified decision or goal episode (D) and observation horizon (H), define:

### Goal-formation causal base: (B_g(D,H))

For operationalization, use a declared universe of **causal units** (U(D,H)). Each unit must have a stable identifier and type, for example an affected agent, an affected population, or a shared condition. Comparisons are made only between units encoded under the same declared scheme.

The subset of causal units for which contemporaneous evidence shows that, **before goal lock-in**:

- their consequences were actually invoked in the reason structure; and
- those consequences had counterfactual relevance — a sufficiently material change in them could in principle have changed the goal selected.

Mere mention, consultation, or symbolic acknowledgement does not establish membership in (B_g).

### Observed effect range: (B_e(D,H))

The subset of the same declared causal-unit universe for which observable evidence within (H) shows a material state change attributable, with the stated level of uncertainty, to the decision or its implementation.

Where heterogeneous units would make direct comparison misleading, report typed gaps separately—for example (G_{agent}), (G_{population}), and (G_{condition})—rather than forcing them into one undifferentiated count.

The Candidate does **not** require a finite agent to anticipate every actual consequence. Instead, define the:

### Causal Inclusion Gap

```math
G(D,H)=B_e(D,H)\setminus B_g(D,H)
```

(G) identifies observed bearers of material consequences that did not have evidenced, counterfactually relevant standing in goal formation.

This makes the claim case-reconstructable rather than equivalent to the trivial statement that finite agents cannot foresee everything.

### Epistemic and privacy boundary

> **Expanding (B_g) means expanding the actor's reason structure, not its entitlement to observe others.**

Unknown consequences remain unknown. A demand to include affected others does not create a right to collect their private information, monitor them, or expand surveillance.

Where material consequences are uncertain and additional observation is not justified:

```math
Unknown + High\ Irreversibility \Rightarrow More\ Precaution / More\ Reversibility
```

not:

```math
Unknown \Rightarrow Permission\ to\ Observe
```

The relevant expansion is in what can count as a reason to alter a goal, not in power over the persons who may be affected.

## 3. M2 — Capability–base mismatch

A causal inclusion gap need not by itself produce catastrophic consequences.

The concern grows when a narrow goal-formation base is coupled to increasing capability, reach, coupling, or action speed. The same omission can matter differently when the actor can change only a local reversible state than when it can rapidly alter many agents' future options.

This Candidate therefore does not claim that advanced capability creates self-first logic. It asks whether advanced capability can give an inherited narrow goal-formation base consequences that exceed the surrounding system's ability to inspect and correct them.

## 4. M3 — Competitive capability-floor externalization

In strategic competition, another actor's capability growth can itself become a risk variable.

Once actor A interprets actor B's capability lead as a threat to A's security, bargaining position, survival, or future agency, the minimum capability investment A regards as acceptable is no longer determined only by A's independent needs. It becomes partly responsive to B's capability level and growth.

Schematically:

```math
C_A^{min}=f(C_B,\Delta C_B,R_A,\ldots)
```

and reciprocally for B.

This can generate:

```math
A\uparrow \Rightarrow Risk_B\uparrow \Rightarrow B\uparrow
\Rightarrow Risk_A\uparrow \Rightarrow A\uparrow \Rightarrow \cdots
```

No greed or hostility is required for this structure. The proposition concerns strategic dependence, not character.

This is related to established security-dilemma and arms-race structures. The proposed extension is not a rediscovery of those literatures; the additional question is how competitive escalation couples to the time available for correction.

## 5. M4 — Unilateral slowdown and its branch point

Competitive systems can create an asymmetry in which the private risk of slowing first is concentrated on the slowing actor while some benefits of restraint are shared.

But unilateral slowdown is **not assumed to fail**.

It may branch:

```math
Unilateral\ Slowdown
\rightarrow
\begin{cases}
Exploitation / Relative\ Loss\\
Reciprocity / De-escalation
\end{cases}
```

A central research question is therefore:

> Under what observable conditions does unilateral restraint become exploitable weakness, and under what conditions does it become a credible signal that produces reciprocal slowdown?

A reliable answer could itself supply part of a second path and therefore must not be excluded in advance.

## 6. M5 — Conditional amplifiers, not the core chain

Capability growth does not universally imply rising total resource consumption. Unit costs can fall and technologies can become more efficient.

A narrower conditional proposition is:

> When relative capability retains strategic value and actors expect unused efficiency gains to translate into relative disadvantage, efficiency gains may be reinvested into scale or the next round of capability growth rather than reducing total resource use.

Thus:

```math
Efficiency\uparrow \not\Rightarrow Total\ Consumption\downarrow
```

and under additional competitive conditions:

```math
Efficiency\uparrow \Rightarrow Scale\uparrow \Rightarrow Total\ Consumption\uparrow
```

may occur.

This is an empirical amplifier, structurally analogous to rebound/Jevons-type effects. It is **not required** for the core V1.7 Candidate mechanism. Evidence against it does not by itself defeat M1, M3, M4, or M6.

## 7. M6 — The two-rate race and reversibility-window compression

Let:

- (T_a^R) denote the effective interval between **consequential actions with material irreversibility or high rollback cost**, not merely the interval between ordinary deployments;
- (T_c) denote the full time required to detect, understand, independently verify, coordinate around, and implement correction of a consequential error.

The conceptual relation is:

```math
W_R=\frac{T_a^R}{T_c}
```

where (W_R) represents a **reversibility-window relation**, not a calibrated probability of catastrophe and not, by itself, an operational risk score.

If consequential hard-to-reverse action intervals shrink while the full correction cycle does not shrink at the same rate, the system moves from:

> action → observe consequences → correct → act again

toward:

> action → act again → act again → consequences become adequately understood later.

### Measurement limitation

(T_c) is not generally observable ex ante. In cases where correction never completes before the relevant window closes, the full value may never be observed at all.

Therefore this Candidate does **not** claim that real-time observation can establish (W_R<1) from (T_c) directly.

Empirical work should instead use explicit proxies or lower-bound observations, such as:

- event or incident → detection interval;
- detection → independent verification interval;
- detection → disclosure interval;
- credible evidence → actual behavioral adjustment interval;
- major deployment → next major deployment interval;
- safety evaluation duration relative to deployment cadence.

If deployment/action intervals repeatedly contract while observable correction-cycle proxies do not contract proportionally, that supports the narrower claim that the **reversibility window is under compression**. It does not establish an exact value of (W_R).

Rapid iteration that remains cheaply and reliably reversible is therefore not, by itself, evidence of dangerous window compression. Empirical tests must report the reversibility or rollback characteristics of the actions whose cadence is being measured.

## 8. Two kinds of threshold

### Stock threshold

A shared resource or condition can be depleted or pushed beyond a material limit. Such thresholds depend on stocks, rates, substitution, new supply, and technological change.

### Time threshold

A different problem occurs when consequential action recurs faster than the system can adequately inspect and correct prior consequences.

This can arise even in a resource-rich system.

The Candidate therefore does not reduce irreversible risk to resource exhaustion. Its central concern is whether the interval available for meaningful correction is shrinking relative to the correction process itself.

> **A narrowing window is not the same as a crossed threshold.**

Crossing remains contingent. The argument is non-fatalistic: the starting logic, competitive mechanism, institutions, restraint, verification practices, or other conditions may change before closure occurs.

## 9. Coupled mechanism under test

R1 does **not** claim that a causal inclusion gap causes strategic competition. Nor does it claim that strategic competition requires a causal inclusion gap. They are analytically separable.

The Candidate instead tests a **coupling hypothesis** between two lines:

### Line A — causal inclusion

```math
Goal\ Formation
\rightarrow Causal\ Inclusion\ Gap\ G(D,H)
\rightarrow Unrepresented\ Consequence\ Exposure
```

### Line B — competitive timing

```math
Strategic\ Competition
\rightarrow Capability\ Escalation\ Pressure
\rightarrow Hard-to-Reverse\ Action\ Interval\ Compression
\rightarrow Correction\ Lag\ Relative\ to\ Action
\rightarrow Reversibility\ Window\ Compression
```

The research question is whether, and under what conditions, the conjunction or interaction of these lines makes irreversible closure more likely or removes effective correction pathways:

```math
Causal\ Inclusion\ Gap
\times Competitive\ Reversibility\ Pressure
\rightarrow ?\ Irreversible\ Closure
```

The multiplication sign denotes **interaction to be tested**, not a validated mathematical product.

This is a coupled hypothesis, not a completed theorem. A successful challenge may show that either line is incorrectly specified, that the lines do not interact, that a missing variable dominates both, that the direction reverses under identifiable conditions, or that a stable self-first mechanism blocks closure.

The resource-rebound proposition in M5 is a conditional amplifier and is intentionally outside the required coupled mechanism.

## 10. Where altruism-first self-interest enters

This Candidate does not define altruism-first self-interest as:

```math
Other>Self
```

or:

```math
Self=0.
```

It proposes a change in (B_g). The breadth of (B_g) alone does not establish V1.6's N: instrumental responsiveness to others is not independent reason-giving standing. A low measured gap must not reclassify a successful self-first mechanism as altruism-first.

Before a goal is locked, consequences for affected others, relationships, shared conditions, and the shared future can have independent causal standing capable of changing what becomes the goal.

This does **not** guarantee cooperation. Whether wider consideration actually changes the self/other boundary, the selected goal, or subsequent conduct remains **OPEN**; awareness is not a demonstrated decision rule.

Seeing another party's consequences does not require choosing that party's preferred outcome. Conflict, defense, refusal, competition, and coercive necessity may remain.

The narrower structural claim is:

> **Cooperation no longer has to wait until after incompatible goals have formed before it can enter as an external constraint.**

And the anti-domination boundary is explicit:

> **The expansion is in the causal range admitted into goal formation, not in the range of authority exercised over individuals.**

## 11. Four research questions

### Q1 — Causal inclusion

Who bears material consequences, and who or what had evidenced, counterfactually relevant standing to change the goal before lock-in?

### Q2 — Escalation

Under what conditions does one actor's capability growth become another actor's effective minimum capability requirement?

### Q3 — Reversibility

Are consequential action intervals contracting faster than the observable processes required for detection, verification, coordination, and correction?

### Q4 — Alternative path

Does a mechanism exist that retains self-first as the prior goal-generation starting point while robustly preventing the preceding dynamics from ending in irreversible closure?

If the answer to Q4 is yes, the necessity/uniqueness claim must be revised rather than explained away.

### Q4 record — Self-first Distributed Reversible-Contract System

**Status: admitted counterexample candidate; mechanism sketch, not a demonstrated A-class result or an established terminal second path.** Source: [PR #7 review 5299991955](https://github.com/Civilization-Leap/Civilization-Leap/pull/7#pullrequestreview-5299991955), submitted through the project account from the supplied adversarial discussion. It is not independent external validation.

The sketch proposes self-first agents using distributed reversible contracts, exit/refusal channels, and suspension or rollback because those protections serve their own interests. It stipulates no permanent supreme controller and allows conflict, capability change, and self-revision. This remains outside N when the reasons are purely instrumental, as V1.6 explicitly permits.

What it establishes now is a serious route to investigate, and a reason not to exclude instrumental protection by definition. The statement “channels remain open if the protocol remains effective” does **not** derive continuing effectiveness. No specified transition model, completed proof, or executable test accompanies that review. It therefore does not yet establish even an observed L3 result. Its proposed bounded target is L2/L3; any L4 extension needs the relevant preservation argument.

The earlier unqualified statement that this sketch had already overturned terminal uniqueness was too strong. This is a correction of the project's assessment, not a withdrawal of the candidate's admission or a change to the frozen A/B conditions. Equally, the sketch's incompleteness is not evidence that the strong direction claim is true.

The next construction must state the horizon and disturbance envelope, agents' decision and revision rules, how enforcement survives or fails, how timely suspension works, and which channels remain effective. It must derive non-closure from those rules. A demonstrated bounded result must be credited against every claim covering that domain; a demonstrated transformation-stable alternative must be credited against the corresponding terminal claim.

**B1–B4 remain unresolved robustness questions**, not additional A requirements: dependence on penalties, capability asymmetry, unilateral-adopter cost, and external identifiability of failure. The current sketch does not justify marking any of these as passed or inevitably failed.

## 12. What would change our mind

This Candidate should be narrowed, revised, or rejected if evidence shows, for example, that:

- (B_g) and (B_e) cannot be reconstructed with sufficient inter-observer reliability to distinguish cases;
- causal inclusion gaps do not predict or explain relevant failures better than simpler variables;
- competitive capability growth does not systematically compress **hard-to-reverse consequential action intervals** in the domains claimed;
- correction processes reliably accelerate at least as fast as consequential action;
- unilateral restraint reliably creates reciprocal restraint under identifiable conditions sufficient to block the proposed recursion;
- or a core/strong counterexample under Section 0 is demonstrated.

No successful counterexample may be absorbed by redefining self-first, N, causal standing, or altruism-first after the fact.

### Open methodological limitations retained in R2

This scope repair does not validate the measurement layer. Pre-lock-in reason coding must be distinguished from retrospective reconstruction; absent or curated records establish an evidence gap, not automatically genuine absence of standing. Aggregating many small decisions into distributed harm requires a declared aggregation model. Neither risk interpretation in M3 nor investment response follows from the self-first label alone. M6 still needs justified proxies, aligned time origins, treatment of incomplete/censored correction episodes, and the actual time remaining before the relevant irreversible boundary; an inter-action interval is not automatically that deadline. Proxy trends alone do not identify the true window or a crossing. These remain open research tasks, not completed tests.

## 13. Compressed statement

> **The research direction is preventing irreversible destruction while keeping shared prosperity possible. It does not predetermine which underlying logic can achieve this.**
>
> **A process success is not automatically a terminal result. A stage equilibrium does not prove preservation across capability and mechanism changes. A valid counterexample remains binding on every claim within its demonstrated scope.**

> **The problem is not merely that goals conflict. A deeper problem may arise when a causal inclusion gap and competitive reversibility pressure coexist: consequential hard-to-reverse action can accelerate while correction remains slower. The interaction is a hypothesis to test, not an assumed causal chain.**
>
> **The required expansion is not greater collective authority over individuals. It is a wider causal reason structure before power becomes purpose.**
>
> **Where consequences cannot legitimately be observed, uncertainty should increase precaution and reversibility—not surveillance.**
>
> **A narrowing reversibility window is a race between rates, not a prediction of a predetermined end-state.**
